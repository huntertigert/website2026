FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What kind of websites do you build?

I design and build fast, responsive websites and web applications, from fully custom React and Tailwind builds to WordPress sites and marketing platform integrations.

Do you build WordPress websites?

Yes. I build WordPress sites when a content platform fits your project better than a custom build, including setups where you can update pages yourself through the WordPress backend. When a custom build is the better fit, I hand-code it with React and Tailwind.

Do you work with HubSpot?

Yes. I connect websites with HubSpot, Salesforce, and Pardot so forms, leads, and data flow into your marketing tools without manual data entry.

Where are you located?

I am a web developer based in Rockwall, Texas. You work with me directly, with no account managers and no relay delays.

What is your process?

Every project follows four steps: discovery (goals, scope, and timeline), design (layouts and direction shaped around your brand and users), build (focused sprints with progress you can see), and launch and support (deployed, tested, and backed by ongoing support if you need it).

Can I update the website myself?

Yes. Sites are built so you can update pages yourself, whether that's through a WordPress backend or a simple custom CMS.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. I offer routine maintenance, hosting, and ongoing support so your site stays fast and secure, on any platform.

Do you use AI in your development work?

Yes. AI-assisted workflows speed up development, which means a faster turnaround and lower cost for you, without cutting corners on quality.

How much does a website cost and how long does it take?

Pricing and timelines depend on the scope of the project, so I don't publish fixed rates. Tell me about your project and I will reply personally, usually within a day, with next steps.

Have a project in mind?

Send me a note about it — I read every message and reply personally, usually within a day.
